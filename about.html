ABOUT

TUCMUN II, the second iteration of John Randolph Tucker High School’s Model United Nations conference based in Henrico, Virginia, is proud to welcome delegates from across the region to a two-day immersive experience in international relations. This year’s conference features a blend of historical, contemporary, and fictional General Assembly, Specialized Agency, and Crisis Simulations committees, with each being designed to challenge delegates to think critically, speak confidently, and collaborate meaningfully. From balancing individual survival with district alliances in the Hunger Games committee to chasing victory in the Roman Tribunal committee, delegates are encouraged to thoroughly immerse themselves in diverse historical and fictional societies to sharpen their skills in diplomacy, negotiation, and problem-solving.

Our theme this year, Ignite the Flame, is a reflection of who we are. It speaks to the fire of curiosity that drives our delegates to research complex issues, the spark of creativity that fuels innovative solutions, and the warmth of community that grows when students come together to learn from one another. It’s a call to action, to light the way forward in a society that needs bold ideas and thoughtful leadership.

With every speech delivered and every resolution debated, we are shaping not only the leaders of tomorrow, but the legacy of today. The flame has been lit, and it will not be extinguished.
