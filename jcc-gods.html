About This Committee

This Joint Crisis Committee Trojan War: Greek Gods will bring together the gods who have influence on the Trojan War. Delegates will debate how much the gods should interfere in the war and whether their actions help bring victory or create more chaos. The committee will also be discussing the agreement made over Helen and decide about if it should be broken and question if divine promises matter more than fairness and balance between the gods and mortals. This is going to be a fast paced committee with changes at every turn and will definitely be something fun to explore.

Topic 1: How Far Should the Gods Go in Shaping the Trojan War

Topic 2: Should the Divine Pact Over Helen Be Broken
