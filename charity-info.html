About Habitat for Humanity

Founded in 1976 in Americus, Georgia, Habitat for Humanity International is a tax-exempt organization that works in partnership with families and volunteers to construct simple, decent, and affordable homes. Its vision is a world where everyone has a safe place to live, and it achieves this by fostering strength, stability, and self-reliance through housing.

Homeowners contribute "sweat equity" by helping build their own homes alongside volunteers, and they pay affordable mortgages, ensuring sustainability and dignity in the process. Over the years, Habitat has expanded to operate in all 50 U.S. states and more than 70 countries, impacting over 62 million people globally. Beyond building homes, the organization also runs programs such as ReStores (nonprofit home improvement stores and donation centers) and Cars for Homes, which allow communities to support Habitat's mission in diverse ways.
