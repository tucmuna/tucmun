About This Committee

The Economic and Financial Committee (ECOFIN) allows delegates to explore how economic policy can support recovery and stability in post-conflict states. In committee, delegates will debate how digital financial tools can be leveraged to accelerate economic recovery, improve financial inclusion, and rebuild trust in damaged economies. This raises questions about accessibility, regulation, and whether digital solutions can realistically support long-term growth. The committee will also explore how strengthening trade and regional economic integration can help post-conflict countries rebuild markets and promote economic cooperation. Delegates will consider how coordinated trade policies and regional partnerships can support sustainable recovery while reducing future economic instability. This committee will definitely involve thoughtful debate, strategic problem-solving, and an engaging discussion throughout.

Topic 1: Leveraging Digital Financial Tools to Accelerate Post-Conflict Economic Recovery

Topic 2: Strengthening Trade and Regional Economic Integration to Support Post-Conflict Improvement
